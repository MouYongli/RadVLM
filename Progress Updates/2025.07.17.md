## 3. Papers read
- "Uncovering Knowledge Gaps in Radiology Report Generation Models
through Knowledge Graphs" (ReXKG)
	- This system extracts information from radiology reports and construct knowledge graphs
	- The paper proposes 3 different metrics to compare the KGs: by node similairty, edge distribution and subgraph coverage
	- Key takeaway: RRG models often produce text that sounds correct but lacks clinical understanding
