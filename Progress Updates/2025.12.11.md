## 2. Technical Details
	
### Reinforcement Learning Overview
- **Goal:** Learn a policy that maximizes cumulative reward  
  \[
  J(\theta)=\mathbb{E}\Big[\sum_{t=0}^{T-1} r_{t+1}\Big]
  \]

- **Key Components**
  - **Agent:** Learns and makes decisions  
  - **Environment:** World the agent interacts with  
  - **State:** Environment snapshot at time \(t\)  
  - **Actions:** Possible moves affecting the next state  
  - **Policy:** Mapping from states to actions (deterministic/stochastic)  
  - **Reward:** Feedback signal  

- **Method Families**
  - **Value-based:** Estimate value → pick action with highest reward
  - **Policy-based:** Directly optimize the policy
  - **Actor–critic:** Learn both value function and policy
  - **Preference-based:** Learn from comparative judgments instead of numeric rewards

### Example algorithms:

#### **REINFORCE**
- **Method:**  Policy-based

- **Formula:**  
	$$ \nabla_\theta J(\theta) = \mathbb{E}_{\pi_\theta}\!\left[\, \sum_{t=0}^{T-1} \nabla_\theta \log \pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)\, G_t \right]$$
	$$\theta \leftarrow \theta + \alpha \nabla_\theta J(\theta)$$

- **Explanation:**  
	REINFORCE increases the likelihood of action sequences that obtain high returns.  
	It is simple and unbiased but has high variance because it relies on Monte-Carlo returns and does not use a critic. Baselines (e.g. expected reward) can be added to reduce variance. If the agent recieves a higher reward than expected, the policy should be changed so that the likelihood for this decision increases.

- **RRG Models**:
	- Xiao, T., Shi, L., Liu, P., Wang, Z., & Bai, C. (2025). **Radiology Report Generation via Multi-objective Preference Optimization.** Proceedings of the AAAI Conference on Artificial Intelligence, 39(8), 8664-8672.

---

#### **SCST (Self-Critical Sequence Training)**
- **Method:**  Policy-based (REINFORCE with a self-critical baseline)

- **Formula:**  
	For a sampled sequence $y^s$ and greedy baseline sequence $\hat{y}$:
	$$\nabla_\theta J(\theta) = \mathbb{E}\!\left[\left(r(y^s) - r(\hat{y})\right)\, \nabla_\theta \log p_\theta(y^s)\right]$$
	
	Loss form (what is minimized):
	$$L_{\text{SCST}}= - \left(r(y^s) - r(\hat{y})\right)\, \log p_\theta(y^s)$$
	

- **Explanation:**  
	SCST modifies REINFORCE by using the model’s own greedy-decoded sequence as a baseline.
	The model samples a sequence and compares its reward to the reward of its greedy output (choosing the next token by highest probability). The difference acts as an advantage: if the sample performs better than the greedy baseline, the model increases its probability; if worse, it decreases it.
	This reduces gradient variance and directly optimizes sequence-level metrics (e.g., CIDEr, BLEU) without requiring a critic network.

- **RRG Models**:
	- Delbrouck, J. B., Chambon, P., Bluethgen, C., Tsai, E., Almusa, O., & Langlotz, C. (2022, December). **Improving the factual correctness of radiology report generation with semantic rewards.** In Findings of the Association for Computational Linguistics: EMNLP 2022 (pp. 4348-4360).
	- Miura, Y., Zhang, Y., Tsai, E., Langlotz, C., & Jurafsky, D. (2021, June). **Improving factual completeness and consistency of image-to-text radiology report generation.** In Proceedings of the 2021 Conference of the North American Chapter of the Association for Computational Linguistics: Human Language Technologies (pp. 5288-5304).

#### **PPO (Proximal Policy Optimization)**
- **Method:**  Actor–critic

- **Formula:**  
	$$L^{\text{PPO}}(\theta) = \mathbb{E}\left[\min\!\left(r_t(\theta) A_t,\;\text{clip}(r_t(\theta), 1-\epsilon, 1+\epsilon) A_t \right)\right]$$
	where
	$$r_t(\theta) = \frac{\pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)}{\pi_{\theta_{\text{old}}}(a_t|s_t)}$$
	and
	$$A_t = Q(s_t​,a_t​)−V(s_t​).$$
	A is the advantage, Q is the action-value function (expected return starting from state and taking action a) and V is the value function (expected return starting from state s).

- **Explanation:**  
	PPO improves policy-gradient methods by preventing large, destabilizing updates. If the action yields a reward better than expected, increase $\pi_{\theta}(a_t, s_t)$ but not too much, else decrease $\pi_{\theta}(a_t, s_t)$ but not too much.
	PPO is one of the most widely used deep RL algorithms because it is stable, sample-efficient, and simple to implement compared to TRPO.

---

#### **RLHF (Reinforcement Learning with Human Feedback)**
- **Method:**  Preference-based method
	
- **Formula:**  

	1. Train a reward model from human preferences
	$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{RM}} = - \mathbb{E}\!\left[\log \sigma\!\left(r_\phi(y^+) - r_\phi(y^-)\right)\right]$$
	2. Optimize the policy using an RL objective (e.g., PPO)
	$$\theta \leftarrow \arg\max_\theta\;\mathbb{E}_{\pi_\theta}\!\left[r_\phi(y)\;-\;\lambda\, \mathrm{KL}\!\left(\pi_\theta(\cdot|x)\;\|\;\pi_{\text{ref}}(\cdot|x)\right)\right]$$

- **Explanation:**  
	RLHF fine-tunes a model by training a reward model on human preference data and then optimizing the policy to maximize this learned reward.
	Because human raters directly specify which outputs are better, the learned reward model approximates human judgment. PPO is most commonly used for the RL phase, with a KL penalty to maintain alignment and prevent reward hacking.

---

#### **DPO (Direct Preference Optimization)**
- **Method:**  Preference-based method 
	
- **Formula:**  

	$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{DPO}}(\theta) = -\mathbb{E}\left[ \log \sigma\left(\beta\left(\log \pi_\theta(y^+|x) - \log \pi_\theta(y^-|x)\right) - \left(\log \pi_{\text{ref}}(y^+|x) - \log \pi_{\text{ref}}(y^-|x)\right)\right)\right]$$


- **Explanation:**  
	DPO directly optimizes the model using pairs of preferred vs. rejected outputs. It pushes the model to increase probability of preferred responses relative to rejected ones.  
	It is widely used for LLM alignment because it avoids reinforcement learning rollouts and is stable, efficient, and easy to train.

- **RRG Models:**
	- Liu, H., Wei, D., Xu, Z., Wu, X., Zheng, Y., & Wang, L. (2025, September). **RRG-DPO: Direct Preference Optimization for Clinically Accurate Radiology Report Generation.** In International Conference on Medical Image Computing and Computer-Assisted Intervention (pp. 552-562). Cham: Springer Nature Switzerland.
	- Zhu, K., Xia, P., Li, Y., Zhu, H., Wang, S., & Yao, H. (2025). **MMedPO: Aligning medical vision-language models with clinical-aware multimodal preference optimization.** In Proceedings of the 42nd International Conference on Machine Learning.
