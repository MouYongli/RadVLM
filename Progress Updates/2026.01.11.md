# 11.01.2026

## 1. Progress Update
- Dataset construction is now fixed
	- During training, the model was returning the prompt itself (e.g., generated reports started with *"Generate a radiology report for these X-rays."*).
	- Root cause: High-resolution images caused the prompt to exceed `max_seq_length`, so the assistant token was sometimes missing.
- Resized images in **p10**
- Started training for **p10**
- Created initial DPO training pipeline
- Created a plan for radiologist preference data curation
	- Generate two reports for ~160 X-ray images from the **p10** test split as a proof of concept  
	  (total test split: 227 images, 67 images reserved as a held-out test set)
	- Assuming **30 seconds per decision** and **3 radiologists**, curation time is **~27 minutes**
	- For final DPO training:
		- Target: **3,600 preference pairs** (full test set: 5,159 images)
		- Estimated effort: **>10 hours per radiologist**
	- Draft email for radiologists:
	```
	Dear XY,

	I am currently conducting a study as part of my Master’s thesis on radiology report generation and would like to invite you to participate.

	In this study, you will be shown a chest X-ray image together with two automatically generated radiology reports. Your task is to select the report that sounds more human-like in terms of grammar, coherence, and writing style. Please do not consider clinical correctness, as this is evaluated separately.

	The goal of this research is to train models that generate radiology reports that are both clinically accurate and linguistically natural, with the long-term aim of supporting radiologists in daily practice.

	The study takes approximately 20–30 minutes. Participation is anonymous, and all data will be used for research purposes only.

	I would greatly appreciate your support and expertise.
	Please feel free to contact me if you are interested or would like further details.
	```
